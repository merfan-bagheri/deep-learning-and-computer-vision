# ⚙️ HW2 Part 2: Optimization, Regularization & Decision Boundaries

Exploration of deep network training dynamics, regularization techniques, and non-linear classification decision boundaries.

## 📌 Covered Concepts
- **Advanced Optimizers**: Implementation and comparative evaluation of Adam, RMSprop, and SGD with Momentum.
- **Regularization & Generalization**: Dropout, L2 weight decay, and batch normalization to mitigate overfitting.
- **Visualizing Decision Boundaries**: Dynamic animations of boundary evolution during training (`mlp_classification_boundaries_example.gif`).

## 📂 Structure
- `HW2_Part2_Optimization_and_Regularization.ipynb`: Benchmark experiments comparing convergence rates and boundary formations.
- `mlp_classification_boundaries_example.gif`: Visual demo of non-linear decision boundary progression across training epochs.
